# Licence FAQ (internal, IT and finance)

## Renewal
The enterprise agreement renews on **2026-12-31**. Renewal is owned by **procurement**: the procurement lead opens the renewal ticket 90 days before the date, and the finance controller signs the purchase order. Finance does not approve renewals; it records them.

## Seat changes
Seats can be added monthly and removed at renewal. A removal takes effect the month after the request is approved.

## Exceptions
Any licence exception above **50 seats** needs two approvals: the requesting **director** and **procurement**. Exceptions below 50 seats need the director's approval only.
